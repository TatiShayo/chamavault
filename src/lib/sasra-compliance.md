# SASRA Compliance Requirements for SACCOs in Kenya

The Sacco Societies Regulatory Authority (SASRA) is the government agency responsible for the registration, regulation, and supervision of SACCO societies in Kenya.

## 1. Minimum Membership Requirements
- A SACCO must have at least **30 members** to be eligible for registration and to maintain its license.
- Members must share a common bond (e.g., occupation, residence, or interest).

## 2. Registration Requirements
- Submission of a formal application to the Commissioner for Co-operative Development.
- Proposed By-laws of the society.
- Minutes of the pre-registration meeting.
- Evidence of payment of the registration fee.
- Fit and proper person forms for proposed directors.

## 3. Annual Returns
- SACCOs are required to file annual returns with SASRA within **four months** after the end of the financial year.
- Returns include audited financial statements, statistical information, and compliance reports.

## 4. Board Composition
The Board of Directors must consist of at least five members, typically including:
- **Chairman**
- **Vice-Chairman** (optional but recommended)
- **Treasurer**
- **Secretary**
- At least **2 other Directors**

Board members must meet "fit and proper" criteria, including integrity and financial stability.

## 5. External Audit Requirements
- Every SACCO must appoint an external auditor approved by SASRA.
- The auditor must conduct an annual audit of the society's financial statements.
- The audited reports must be presented to the members at the Annual General Meeting (AGM) and submitted to SASRA.

## 6. Licensing (For Deposit-Taking SACCOs)
- Deposit-taking SACCOs (DT-SACCOs) require a specific license from SASRA.
- Non-withdrawable Back Office Service Activity (NWDT) SACCOs must also be registered and regulated if they meet certain thresholds (e.g., holding deposits above KES 100 million).
